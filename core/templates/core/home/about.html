About E-Fix Hub

We help communities turn discarded electronics into valuable, repaired, and reusable technology.

Our Story

A smarter way to manage e-waste.

E-Fix Hub was built to solve a growing global problem: electronic waste. Millions of devices are discarded every year even when they can still be repaired, refurbished, or repurposed.

Our platform connects customers, agents, and mechanics in one system so broken devices can be collected, repaired, and either resold or reused instead of ending up in landfill.

What We Do

Collect

We make it easy for users to upload and hand over damaged devices for review and collection.

Repair

Certified mechanics restore items and record repair activity to maintain quality standards.

Resell

Verified refurbished products are published back into the market and sold responsibly.
